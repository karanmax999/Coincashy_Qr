# Coincashy Blog – Content & Compliance Policy (v1, draft for sign-off)

Derived from the site's existing **Terms of Service** (§5: content is informational/educational and is not investment advice
or a recommendation within the meaning of the EU Market Abuse Regulation) and the **Risk Warning & Disclaimer** page.
Every automated draft is checked against this policy, and a named human approver signs off every post before it goes live.

> Status: DRAFT – to be confirmed by the Coincashy approver(s) before automation is switched on.

## 1. Hard rules (any breach = post is not published)
1. **No investment advice.** Never recommend buying, selling or holding any crypto-asset. No price predictions, targets,
   "best time to buy", or "you should". (Disclaimer: "No Investment Advice"; Terms §5.)
2. **No promises.** No guaranteed returns, "risk-free", "safe investment", "instant profit", "zero risk", "always", "never lose".
3. **Risk is always stated.** Every post carries the site disclaimer (added automatically by the blog template) and, where
   relevant to the topic, a plain-language risk sentence in the body.
4. **Regulatory status – only what is true and on the site.** Describe Coincashy only as on the About/Compliance pages
   (Coincashy Sp. z o.o., Warsaw, KRS 0001135823, registered VASP). Do not imply a licence, approval, deposit insurance
   or compensation-scheme cover that does not exist. Crypto-assets are not covered by the FSCS/FOS (Disclaimer).
5. **Availability.** Never imply a service is available everywhere. Use: "availability depends on your country and
   eligibility". UK: services only for exempt categories under the UK financial promotion regime (Disclaimer) – do not
   write UK-targeted promotional content.
6. **No unannounced partners or products.** Do not name partners, banks, card issuers or features that are not already
   public on coincashy.io. Roadmap items need written approval.
7. **Facts need sources.** Any statistic, regulation or third-party fact cites a reputable source (regulator, official
   issuer page, major outlet). Prefer primary sources (ESMA, EBA, FCA, FinCEN, FINTRAC, issuers' own pages).
8. **No copying.** Original wording only; quotes under 15 words, attributed.
9. **No competitor disparagement.** Comparisons must be factual and sourced.
10. **Fees and pricing** only as shown on /fees at the time of writing; link to /fees rather than restating numbers.

## 2. Style
- English (British spelling), plain language, short paragraphs, active voice. Explain jargon on first use.
- Tone: calm, trustworthy, practical. No hype words ("moon", "explode", "skyrocket", "massive", "guaranteed").
- Audience per post: individuals OR businesses – pick one and say so in the brief.

## 3. Required elements per post
- Title ≤ 65 characters, meta description 140–155 characters, one focus keyword.
- Key takeaways box at the top (`<div class="key-takeaways">`).
- 2–4 internal links to relevant Coincashy pages or articles; one CTA to the matching product page or /contact.
- 3–5 FAQ entries (short answers), sources list, author and reviewer.
